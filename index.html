Your privacy

CNC LightBit LLC only collects what we need to make and deliver your order.

What we collect

  • Your email address, and your name and phone number if you give them
  • Your shipping address, if you choose shipping
  • Your order details and any artwork you upload

How we use it

  • To make your order, send your invoice and proofs, and ship or arrange pickup
  • To answer your questions

We never sell, rent or share your information for marketing. It's only shared with the services that help us fill your order: our website host (Netlify), our email (Google), our invoicing (Wave) and the carrier that delivers your package (USPS).

Payments

This website never asks for or stores card or bank details. You pay through a secure invoice from Wave. Pay by bank transfer or cash and save 3%. Credit and debit card payments are the regular price.

Security

Everything you send through this site is encrypted (https). We regularly delete old order requests once they're filled.
